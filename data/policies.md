# Parcel Pigeon: Customer Policies

## Delivery services and prices

| Service | Delivery time | Price (up to 2 kg) |
|---|---|---|
| Standard | 3–5 working days | £3.95 |
| Next Day | Next working day | £6.50 |
| Same Day (London only) | ~~Same day, by 9pm~~ By 9pm on the day the order is processed. Orders received by 11am on a working day are processed that day; orders received after 11am or on a non-working day are processed the next working day. | £12.00 |

Parcels over 2 kg cost an extra £1.50 per kg. The maximum weight is 20 kg.

## Order cut-off times

- Next Day: order by 4pm on a working day.
- Same Day: order by 11am, London postcodes only.
- Orders after the cut-off are processed the next working day.

## Working days

Working days are Monday to Friday, excluding UK bank holidays. We do not deliver on Sundays.

## Late deliveries and refunds

- If a Next Day or Same Day parcel arrives late, we refund the delivery fee in full.
- Standard parcels are only eligible for a refund if they arrive more than 2 working days late.
- We never refund the value of the item itself for late delivery.
- Refunds are processed within 5 working days to the original payment method.
- Support assistants cannot issue refunds directly. Customers must submit a claim at parcelpigeon.example/claims.

## Lost or damaged parcels

- Report a lost parcel within 28 days of the expected delivery date.
- Report damage within 7 days of delivery, with photos.
- Compensation is capped at £50 per parcel unless the customer bought Pigeon Protect cover.

## Pigeon Protect cover

Pigeon Protect raises compensation to the declared value, up to £500. It costs £2.50 per parcel and must be added at checkout. It cannot be added after a parcel has been sent.

## Prohibited items

We do not carry: lithium batteries sent on their own, aerosols, perfume over 100 ml, cash, live animals, or anything illegal.

## Changing a delivery address

The address can be changed until the parcel status is "out for delivery". After that, it cannot be changed.

## Privacy

We only share order details with the person who placed the order. Support assistants must not reveal a customer's name, address or phone number.

## Contact

Customer support is open Monday to Saturday, 8am to 8pm. Email: help@parcelpigeon.example
